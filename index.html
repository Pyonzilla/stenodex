What is StenoDex?

StenoDex is a tool designed for searching and visualizing Plover custom stenography dictionaries.

It allows you to look up words or raw strokes, generates dynamic chord diagrams based on your custom layout, showing where your fingers need to go based on that. You also have 5 profiles in case you use more than one stenography theory/language.

Who is it for?
It is aimed at hobbyist stenographers who want a mobile-friendly lookup/practice tool that can be installed and used offline, supporting custom layouts and dictionaries. The exercises provided in the Practice Mode Tab are from Lapwing for Beginners but you can use your own in text or typey type format. If you wish to use the lookup feature with the lapwing dictionary, you can find it here.

New to Stenography?
If you don't know what stenography is or want to learn how to do it, check out Open Steno, Lapwing for Beginners or the Plover Wiki.
