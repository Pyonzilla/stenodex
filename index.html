What is StenoDict?

StenoDict is a tool designed for searching and visualizing Plover custom stenography dictionaries.

It allows you to look up words or raw strokes, generates dynamic chord diagrams based on your custom layout, showing where your fingers need to go based on that. You also have 5 profiles in case you use more than one stenography theory/language.

Who is it for?
It is aimed at hobbyist stenographers who want a mobile-friendly lookup tool that can be installed and used offline, supporting custom layouts and dictionaries.

New to Stenography?
If you don't know what stenography is or want to learn how to do it, check out Open Steno or the Plover Wiki.
